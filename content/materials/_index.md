title: Materials
description: Slide decks, worksheets and native-voice audio for every unit.
---

Every unit of this course ships with three artefacts: an editable slide deck, a
printable worksheet, and audio. They are generated locally from the shared
`slidegen` and `sheetgen` LaTeX templates and from the `audiogen` Piper
workflow, then **committed** to the repository — continuous integration only
verifies that what a page links to actually exists and is attributed. Nothing
here needs a TeX installation to use.

**Formats.** Slide decks are published as `.odp` and PDF, worksheets as PDF.
Open formats are a requirement, not a preference: a teacher must be able to cut
a slide, change an example, or reprint a worksheet with a different font size.

**Typography.** Both LaTeX templates are set with an OpenType face carrying
complete Greek coverage, so the tonos, the diaeresis and final sigma render
correctly in decks and worksheets as well as on the site.

**Audio.** Vocabulary, dialogues and texts are synthesised with the openly
licensed Greek Piper voice `el_GR-rapunzelina-low` and committed as OGG/Opus.
This is currently the only open Greek voice available to the project, and it is
a single speaker: dialogues therefore mark their turns typographically rather
than acoustically. The audio will be re-synthesised if a better open Greek voice
appears.

Downloads appear once at the foot of each unit page, next to the unit they
belong to. The two sections below list the committed files directly.
