{{< lead >}}
A2 is where Greek stops being a set of present-tense phrases and becomes a
language with a past. The aorist arrives in Unit 1 and is not finished with
until Unit 12; alongside it come the future, the genitive, and the everyday
transactions — a ferry ticket, a doctor's appointment, a phone call — that
require them.
{{< /lead >}}
{{< /hero >}}

## What changes at A2

Three things, and they are related:

1. **Tense.** The aorist is the workhorse past tense of spoken Greek, and its
   stem is not predictable from the present for a large class of common verbs
   (γράφω → έγραψα, but βλέπω → είδα). A2 introduces it early and returns to it
   repeatedly rather than presenting it once as a table.
2. **Case.** The genitive completes the case system a learner actually needs at
   this level: possession, dates, and the weak pronoun forms (μου, σου, του)
   that appear in almost every sentence of natural speech.
3. **Length.** Texts stop being three lines long. The final unit works on short
   narratives — a holiday, an incident, a childhood memory — which is the first
   time the learner has to sustain a past-tense text across a paragraph.

## The twelve units

| # | Unit | Focus | Skills |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Τι έκανες χθες; | The aorist of regular verbs; time expressions for the past | speaking_production |
| 2 | Ανώμαλοι αόριστοι | The frequent irregular aorists: είδα, ήπια, πήγα, είπα, έφαγα | language_awareness |
| 3 | Θα πάμε στη θάλασσα | The future with θα; simple and continuous future | writing |
| 4 | Η γενική | The genitive: possession, dates, and the weak personal pronouns | language_awareness |
| 5 | Ταξίδι στα νησιά | Ferries and timetables: buying a ticket, asking about departures | reading, speaking_interaction |
| 6 | Στον γιατρό | Health: symptoms, the body, an appointment, a pharmacy | speaking_interaction |
| 7 | Η δουλειά μου | Work: jobs, workplaces, describing what one does and did | speaking_production |
| 8 | Ο καιρός | Weather: a forecast, the seasons, the meltémi | listening |
| 9 | Μια πρόσκληση | Invitations: inviting, accepting, declining politely, apologising | writing, speaking_interaction |
| 10 | Στο τηλέφωνο | On the telephone: opening, asking for someone, taking a message | listening, speaking_interaction |
| 11 | Μεγαλύτερος, καλύτερος | Comparatives and superlatives; comparing places, people and prices | language_awareness |
| 12 | Μια μικρή ιστορία | Short narrative: sequencing a past-tense account across a paragraph | writing |

## Exam

A2 closes with its own exam bundle on the same pattern as A1 — reading,
listening, writing and speaking, with the printable paper under
`static/downloads/a2/` and the grade boundary stated on the page.

## Curriculum mapping

A2 unit can-dos map to `A2.{DOMAIN}.{SCALE}.{SEQ}` descriptor IDs, adding the
transaction and correspondence scales that A1 only touched: buying goods and
services, exchanging information, notes and messages. As at A1, cells with no
official A2 descriptor are recorded as `no-official-descriptor` in
`conformance.yml` rather than dropped.
