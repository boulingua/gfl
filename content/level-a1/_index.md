## Entry condition

A1 assumes [Level 0](/level-0/). Every Greek word on these pages is printed in
Greek script with its tonos; there is no transliteration column and no
Latin-alphabet fallback. A learner who cannot yet decode μπαίνω or αυτοκίνητο
aloud should finish Level 0 first — it takes about a fortnight and it makes the
rest of the course possible.

## The recurring cast

Four people carry the course from A1 to B1, so that vocabulary and situations
accumulate rather than reset each unit:

- **Ελένη** — 26, from Thessaloniki, has just moved to Athens for work.
- **Νίκος** — her neighbour, runs the kafeneío on the corner.
- **Μαρία** — 63, sells vegetables at the Wednesday λαϊκή.
- **Γιώργος** — 19, studies in Athens, works weekends on the ferries.

## The twelve units

| # | Unit | Focus | Skills |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Γεια σου! | Greeting, taking leave, γεια σου / γεια σας; giving one's name and origin | speaking_interaction |
| 2 | Από τα γράμματα στις λέξεις | Consolidating Level 0 on real words: signs, names, the first hundred nouns | reading |
| 3 | Αριθμοί | Numbers 0–100; prices, telephone numbers, house numbers | listening |
| 4 | Τι ώρα είναι; | Telling the time; days of the week; opening hours | listening, speaking_interaction |
| 5 | Η οικογένειά μου | Family vocabulary; possessives μου, σου, του; describing people | speaking_production |
| 6 | Στο καφενείο | Ordering; ένας / μία / ένα; παρακαλώ and ευχαριστώ in use | speaking_interaction |
| 7 | Φαγητό και ποτό | Food and drink; reading a short menu; likes and dislikes | reading, speaking_production |
| 8 | Στη λαϊκή | The street market: quantities, weights, haggling politely | speaking_interaction |
| 9 | Πού είναι…; | Asking and giving directions; prepositions of place; the city | speaking_interaction |
| 10 | Η μέρα μου | Daily routine; present tense of -ω verbs; frequency adverbs | writing, speaking_production |
| 11 | Ουσιαστικά, γένος, άρθρα | The three genders; the definite and indefinite articles; noun endings | language_awareness |
| 12 | Ονομαστική και αιτιατική | Nominative and accusative: subject, object, and after prepositions | language_awareness, writing |

Units 11 and 12 come last deliberately. The gender system and the first two
cases are easier to state once learners have met a few hundred nouns in
context; the earlier units use the forms, and the last two make the pattern
explicit.

## Exam

A1 closes with an exam bundle — a sibling page of the last unit, carrying its
own duration, point total and grade boundary, with the printable paper under
`static/downloads/a1/`. Exams are first-class pages in this course, not
appendices: they are readable in the browser and downloadable as PDF.

## Curriculum mapping

Every unit declares its can-do statements and maps them to descriptor IDs of
the shared [curriculum framework](https://github.com/boulingua/curriculum) in
the form `A1.{DOMAIN}.{SCALE}.{SEQ}` — reception, production, interaction, the
linguistic-competence scales, and the sociolinguistic and pragmatic scales. For
the cells the Companion Volume leaves empty at A1 — much of mediation and the
plurilingual scales — the course records `no-official-descriptor` rather than
omitting them silently.
